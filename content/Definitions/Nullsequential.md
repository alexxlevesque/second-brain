*(adj.)* Describing an event that occurs within a temporal sequence but produces no causal, material, or narrative consequence on the external world's subsequent state — the world unfolds identically to how it would have if the event had never happened.

Distinct from *inconsequential*: nullsequential is a structural, causal claim about whether an event propagates forward into outcomes, while inconsequential is a subjective claim about whether an event carries internal or felt weight. The two properties are independent, producing four possible combinations for any given event:

- **Nullsequential + inconsequential** — no forward causal thread, no internal charge (e.g., what you had for lunch three Tuesdays ago)
- **Nullsequential + consequential** — no forward causal thread, but real internal charge (e.g., a work presentation that won't affect your career but still costs you ego)
- **Sequential + inconsequential** — real forward causal thread, but no felt weight (e.g., a routine email that quietly triggers decisions above your head)
- **Sequential + consequential** — real forward causal thread and real felt weight

Antonym: *sequential.*
