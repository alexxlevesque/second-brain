Definition: Conformity is ==the act of matching your attitudes, beliefs, and behaviours to group norms, social standards, or authority==. It involves altering your actions to fit in, gain acceptance, or adhere to established rules and expectations.

A successful engineer must be creative. An engineer that conforms to norms cannot be creative. Therefore, a conforming engineer cannot be a successful engineer.
