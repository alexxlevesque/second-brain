Take a dentist whose portfolio returns 15% a year with 10% volatility. Over a full year, he has about a 93% chance of being up. Over a single minute or second, it's barely better than a coin flip. If he checks prices constantly, he sees mostly noise. Because losses hurt more than gains feel good, frequent checking makes him miserable even though his strategy is excellent. The shorter your observation window, the more of what you see is randomness rather than signal.
