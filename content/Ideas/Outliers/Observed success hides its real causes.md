We explain success with the visible individual — their talent, drive, genius — while the real drivers are often hidden: birth date, cultural legacy, family wealth, and lucky timing. Gladwell argues the "self-made" story is a confounded one; the outlier is inseparable from the context that produced them. This is the everyday version of the statistical warning that [[Correlation does not imply causation]].
