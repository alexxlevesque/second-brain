Raw IQ has diminishing returns; what separates outliers is "practical intelligence" — knowing how to read a situation, assert your needs, and work institutions to your advantage. Gladwell (drawing on Annette Lareau) argues this is not innate but taught: middle-class "concerted cultivation" trains children to negotiate with authority, while poorer children are taught deference. It is a learned cultural legacy that quietly reproduces advantage.
