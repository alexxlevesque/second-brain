Patterns of behavior get passed down as cultural legacies that persist long after the conditions that created them are gone. Gladwell's cases: high "power distance" in a cockpit hierarchy contributing to plane crashes, and the persistence and hard work of rice-paddy agriculture correlating with strong mathematics performance. Where we come from quietly programs how we act, often below awareness.
