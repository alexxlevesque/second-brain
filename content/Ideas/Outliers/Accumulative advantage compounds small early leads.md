Small initial advantages snowball into large ones — the "Matthew Effect." Gladwell's example: children born early in the selection year for youth hockey are slightly bigger, get picked for elite teams, receive more coaching and ice time, and pull further ahead. The early edge was arbitrary, but the system compounds it into real, lasting skill differences.
