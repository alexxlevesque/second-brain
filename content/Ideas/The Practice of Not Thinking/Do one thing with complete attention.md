Single-tasking is a spiritual practice: eat while only eating, walk while only walking. Full attention on one simple act starves the wandering mind and trains presence far better than any abstract effort to "be mindful." Multitasking, by contrast, feeds agitation. Compare habit design's [[Make it easy with the two-minute rule]] — both shrink attention down to one doable thing.
