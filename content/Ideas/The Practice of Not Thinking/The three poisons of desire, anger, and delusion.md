Drawing on Buddhism, Koike names three mental poisons behind unhappiness: desire (craving more), anger (aversion and irritation), and delusion (confusion and uncertainty). Most negative thoughts trace back to one of these. Noticing *which* poison is active in a given moment lets you meet it with awareness instead of unconsciously feeding it.
