Much suffering is generated in speech driven by the craving to be validated — we talk to be liked and half-listen while preparing our reply. Koike treats speaking less and more honestly, and listening completely, as a discipline that quiets both the ego and the mind's noise. Real listening is itself a form of returning to the present.
