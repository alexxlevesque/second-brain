The antidote to runaway thought is to drop out of the head and into the senses. Deliberately attending to what you see, hear, smell, taste, and touch anchors you in the present, where suffering-by-rumination cannot follow. The senses are always in the now; thought rarely is. This is the practical twin of Glasser's insistence to [[Focus on the present, not the past]].
