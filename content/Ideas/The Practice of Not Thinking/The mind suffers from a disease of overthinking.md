Koike's premise: the modern mind is sick with excess thought. We compulsively replay the past and rehearse the future, and because the brain weights the negative, most of this chatter breeds anxiety and dissatisfaction. The key move is recognizing thinking *itself* as the problem — not the content of any single thought — which is the first step toward relief.
