Quantitative easing swaps one asset for another: the central bank buys bonds and pays with newly created reserves. The private sector's total wealth is roughly unchanged — it just holds reserves instead of bonds. QE works mainly through *portfolio rebalancing* (pushing investors toward riskier assets) and *signaling* (committing to low rates), not by dropping spendable cash into the real economy. See how this depends on [[Bank reserves never leave the banking system]].
