"You do not rise to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your systems." Goals set a direction but are shared by winners and losers alike — what separates them is the process. Focusing on the system (the repeatable daily actions) rather than the outcome makes progress automatic and removes the all-or-nothing dependence on a finish line.
