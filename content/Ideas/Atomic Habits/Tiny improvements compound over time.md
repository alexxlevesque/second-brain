Getting 1% better every day makes you ~37x better over a year; 1% worse compounds toward zero. Habits are the compound interest of self-improvement, but the results are delayed — you cross a "plateau of latent potential" where effort seems wasted before the breakthrough. This is the personal-development version of [[Accumulative advantage compounds small early leads]] and the daily mechanism behind [[The 10,000-hour rule of mastery]].
