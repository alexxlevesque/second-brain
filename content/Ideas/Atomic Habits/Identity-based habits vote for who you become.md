The most durable way to change is to focus on *who you wish to become* rather than what you want to achieve. Every action is a vote for a type of person: each time you write, you become a writer; each workout, an athlete. Behavior that conflicts with your self-image doesn't last, so lasting change is identity change from the inside out.
