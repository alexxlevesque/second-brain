Every habit runs on a loop of cue → craving → response → reward, which yields four levers for building good habits: make it **obvious**, **attractive**, **easy**, and **satisfying**. Invert each to break a bad habit: make it invisible, unattractive, difficult, and unsatisfying. The framework turns vague willpower into concrete design choices.
