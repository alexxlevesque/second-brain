Perfection isn't required; consistency is. Missing once is an accident, but missing twice is the start of a new (bad) habit. The rule "never miss twice" protects momentum without demanding an unbroken streak — it lets you be human while keeping the identity intact. Showing up imperfectly beats the all-or-nothing thinking that quietly kills most habits.
