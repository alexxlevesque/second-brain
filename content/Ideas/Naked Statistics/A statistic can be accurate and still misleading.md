Precision is not the same as truth. A number can be computed correctly yet paint a false picture — reporting the mean when a few outliers skew it (vs. the median), citing a percentage change without the base rate, or choosing the time window that flatters your case. Descriptive statistics compress reality, and every act of compression is also a choice about what to hide.
