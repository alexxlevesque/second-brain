Mental health, for Glasser, is the ability to meet your own needs in a way that doesn't stop others from meeting theirs — that is his working definition of responsibility. Symptoms are reframed as irresponsible ways of coping, and the cure is learning more responsible behavior rather than assigning blame or a diagnosis.
