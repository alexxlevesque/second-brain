Reality Therapy deliberately refuses the hunt for causes in childhood. The past is fixed and offers nothing you can act on; only present behavior can change. So therapy asks "What are you doing now, and is it working?" rather than "Why are you like this?" This mirrors the Buddhist practice to [[Return to the present through the five senses]].
